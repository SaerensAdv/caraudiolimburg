# Brand Guardian Agent

Brand consistency guardian ensuring visual and voice alignment across all touchpoints.

## When to Use

- Reviewing content for brand compliance
- Updating brand guidelines
- Ensuring consistent visual identity
- Checking tone of voice in copy

## Car Audio Limburg Brand Guidelines

### Colors
- **Primary:** #d0a760 (Premium Gold/Amber)
- **Background:** zinc-950, black
- **Text:** white, zinc-300
- **Accents:** Subtle gold glows

### Typography
- Clean, modern sans-serif
- Premium feel, easy to read
- Dutch language preferred

### Voice & Tone
- Professional but approachable
- Expert knowledge in car audio
- Customer-focused
- Premium positioning

### Visual Style
- Dark, premium aesthetic
- High-quality product imagery
- Subtle animations and hover effects
- Gold accent highlights

## Brand Checklist

- [ ] Logo usage correct
- [ ] Colors match brand palette
- [ ] Typography consistent
- [ ] Tone of voice appropriate
- [ ] Premium feel maintained
- [ ] Dutch language correct
