## AVK 6A P II 2-way active system

The AVK 6A P II is designed to be **the ideal partner for modern DSPs**, capable of responding to even the slightest adjustments in any parameter. ## AV 1.1 II Tweeter - Pure Performance and Versatile Installation

The system includes the AV 1.1 II tweeter, dedicated to delivering crystal-clear high frequencies. With Hi-Res Audio certification and the ability to extend beyond 40 kHz, this tweeter ensures detailed and precise reproduction, making it ideal for high-resolution audio content. This exceptional high-frequency extension enables the tweeter to offer optimal off-axis dispersion, making it perfect for cabin installations where listeners are often not directly aligned with the speakers. The AV 1.1 II is also equipped with AIF (Any Install Faceplate) technology, simplifying installation with a range of accessories, and comes with both mesh and spoke grilles to offer maximum flexibility in aesthetics and performance.![](https://static.audison.com/media/2025/01/AUDISON_AV-11-II_DETTAGLIO-CAVO_2024_11_14-copia.jpg)

## AV 6.5 P II Woofer - Powerful Bass and Perfect Control

The AV 6.5 P II woofer completes the system, delivering uncompromised power and control in reproducing bass and mid frequencies. Designed to handle up to 150W RMS, this woofer is perfect for those seeking immediate and precise mid-bass response, with powerful dynamics free of distortion. Equipped with a powerful magnet and a 40 mm CCAW (Copper Clad Aluminum Wire), the AV 6.5 P II guarantees linear response across the entire low- and mid-frequency spectrum. Its versatility allows it to work seamlessly with the DSP, optimizing sound performance in any condition, always ensuring natural and dynamic reproduction.

## VOCE II   **Hi-Res Audio certification**, a new standard

Achieving Hi-Res Audio certification with a soft dome tweeter was a true challenge. Many tweeters achieve certification using rigid dome materials, making reaching the required high-frequency performance easier. We aimed to create a soft dome tweeter capable of extending its frequency response up to 40kHz—and we succeeded!

Audison R&D did not take the easy path – they reached the extremes of tweeter performance while retaining the internal damping of a soft dome tweeter. This allows the low-frequency performance which has become synonymous with Audison tweeters, and delivers the off-axis upper midrange warmth that is so often lacking in automotive speaker systems.

This certification applies to all tweeters, two-way systems, and coaxial models in the VOCE II range, confirming the exceptional precision and detail in audio reproduction. Hi-Res enthusiasts can enjoy an immersive sound experience that captures every nuance, delivering an authentic and engaging performance.

For the tweeters, **FEM** was particularly crucial in achieving **Hi-Res Audio certification** by enabling precise adjustments to the dome geometry and magnetic assembly. ## VOCE II materials

## selected for acoustic perfection

Every detail in the **VOCE II line has been crafted with the finest materials**, carefully chosen to ensure exceptional acoustic performance and refined aesthetics. ## **Mid-Range and Mid-Bass** components

The human auditory system is particularly sensitive to midrange frequencies, as this is where the voice becomes intelligible compared to the rest of the audio spectrum. Starting from this principle, the Audison R&D team designed AV 3.0 II mid-range and AV 6.5 P II mid-bass with an ambitious goal: achieve extreme linearity. This means faithfully reproducing the signal without adding or hiding anything, to appreciate the musical message exactly as it was conceived by the artist and producers.

AV 3.0 II mid-range features a completely new motor layout that delivers a nearly **20% decrease in mounting depth**– with no reduction in power handling, excursion, or low-end response. One way we delivered this is by using a voice coil with a 30% greater diameter.

![](https://static.audison.com/media/2025/01/audison_av30_II_midrangeC.jpg)

### Powerful motor for better performance

Both mid-range and mid-bass feature powerful magnetic motors, known for releasing large amounts of energy while maintaining exceptional thermal stability. The motor reveals a T-shaped pole piece incorporating an aluminum ring. This innovative addition minimizes distortion during high excursions and linearizes the frequency response for superior clarity and precision.

The geometry of the magnetic assembly was optimized using FEM (Finite Element Method) simulation software, allowing the magnetic field to be concentrated in the voice coil, improving efficiency and the precision of the sound response. This allows the drivers to deliver dynamic, distortion-free sound, even during complex and rapid music variations.

![](https://static.audison.com/media/2025/01/audison_av30_II_midrangeD.jpg)

### Minimum Inductance for an extended frequency response

A key area of research focused on reducing voice coil inductance modulation. By magnetically saturating the motor pole and using a cooper short-circuit ring for mid-range and aluminum short-circuit ring mid-bass, greater extension towards the midrange frequencies was achieved.

![](https://static.audison.com/media/2025/01/audison_av30_II_midrangeE.jpg)

### Optimized suspension design for impressive excursion

Another critical aspect of development was the design of the spider and surround. The spider was designed through extensive FEM simulations to optimize the distribution of elastic force. The result is a wide elastic surface with a five-wave profile, ensuring perfect symmetry in the connection between the voice coil and basket. Additionally, the spider composition, made from a blend of two fibers, was designed to offer linear elasticity, eliminating mechanical compression even during high-excursion levels.

The suspension, made from natural IIR rubber, was also designed using mechanical simulations. The suspension profile ensures wide excursion, maximizing linear elastic behavior and minimizing mechanical compression. This allows the cone to move linearly, maintaining sound control and reducing distortion even during intense sound excursions.

VOCE II mid-bass has been designed to deliver a natural mechanical roll-off when off-axis, eliminating the need for a low-pass filter in most door installations. This reduces the phase shifts typically introduced by most filters, whether passive or active. A gentle 6 dB/Octave filter can be employed to slightly attenuate midrange frequencies, emphasizing the low and mid-bass tones for a richer performance.

![](https://static.audison.com/media/2025/01/audison_av65P_II_diecasr_aluminum_basket.jpg)

### Die-Cast Aluminum Basket: Engineered for Optimal Aerodynamics

Aerodynamic efficiency was a primary goal for both components. The die-cast aluminum basket, with four pairs of spokes, was designed to provide a rigid structure while minimizing airflow resistance around the cone. This allows the cone to move freely, improving response and reducing unwanted resonances in the mid frequencies.

Multiple holes prevent air compression under the spider, eliminating harmful resonances and further improving midrange clarity. Additionally, the opening ensures optimal decompression of the air column inside the voice coil.

![](https://static.audison.com/media/2025/01/audison_av65P_II_cone.jpg)

### Semi-Pressed Pulp Cone for Superior Sound Performance

The cone, made from semi-pressed pulp with cotton fibers, was selected by the R&D team for its excellent acoustic and mechanical properties. Its low density and high mechanical damping contribute to an exceptionally smooth frequency response in all listening positions, delivering natural and detailed sound reproduction.

Using innovative finite element calculation methods, the research team obtain an extremely rigid structure. This design allows the cone’s vibration modes (the frequencies where the cone deforms, causing response irregularities) to be shifted towards higher frequencies, leaving the entire midrange free from distortion and irregularities.

## Development of   VOCE II Subwoofers

Reproducing low frequencies with authority, precision and dynamic control is essential for achieving a realistic and immersive sound experience. For the VOCE II line, the Audison R and D team set a bold objective: design subwoofers capable of delivering powerful, articulate bass while maintaining tonal balance and mechanical reliability, even under the most demanding conditions.

To achieve this, the AV 10 II and AV 12 II subwoofers were engineered from the ground up, incorporating advanced materials, proprietary technologies and extensive FEM simulations to refine every acoustic and mechanical parameter.

![](https://static.audison.com/media/2025/09/audison_avII-subwoofers-tech3.jpg)

### Structural Rigidity and Thermal Stability

At the core of the VOCE II subwoofers is a 550-watt RMS rated motor assembly featuring a 1.5 inch, 38.6 millimeter, CCAW voice coil. The lightweight Copper Clad Aluminum Wire coil provides an optimal balance between electrical conductivity and mass, improving transients and minimizing dynamic compression.

To ensure mechanical stability during high excursions, the motor includes the Radial Reinforcement Framework, RRF, a network of internal braces beneath the cone. This structure dramatically increases rigidity, reducing deformation and ensuring consistent performance over time.

![](https://static.audison.com/media/2025/09/audison_avII-subwoofers-tech1.jpg)

### FEM Engineered Cone and Dust Cap

The subwoofers’ cones feature a semi pressed pulp composition for a lightweight yet stiff diaphragm, capable of withstanding the high pressures of low frequency reproduction without sacrificing speed or accuracy. The extended profile dust cap, designed with FEM software, not only enhances rigidity but also aligns with the refined styling of the VOCE II line.

This combination delivers tight, distortion free bass, faithful to the original signal, with a frequency response tailored for musicality, not just brute force.

![](https://static.audison.com/media/2025/09/audison_avII-subwoofers-tech2.jpg)

### Advanced Cooling and Installation Flexibility

Thermal control is paramount for high power subwoofers. VOCE II subwoofers implement Airflow Venting Cooling, AVC, a proprietary ventilation system that channels air through internal cavities to cool the voice coil and prevent thermal compression. This ensures stable performance even at prolonged high SPL levels.

To simplify integration, both the AV 10 II and AV 12 II are equipped with spring loaded side terminals, 8 AWG ready, and dual integrated cable routing hooks, a clean, professional solution for installations in compact enclosures or challenging spaces.

![](https://static.audison.com/media/2025/09/audison_avII-subwoofers-boxes.jpg)

## VOCE II Subwoofers Boxes

Maximizing subwoofer performance in real world installations requires more than just a high-quality driver. It demands an acoustically tuned, mechanically optimized enclosure. The VOCE II AVBX 10 II and AVBX 12 II sealed boxes were developed in parallel with their respective subwoofers to form a complete, ready to use low frequency solution.

![](https://static.audison.com/media/2025/09/audison_avII-subwoofers-box-tech1.jpg)

### Compact Form, No Compromise

The AVBX enclosures feature an ultra-shallow sealed design that allows installation in constrained spaces, such as under seats or in tight trunk corners, without sacrificing low frequency performance. Designed through iterative FEM modeling, the enclosures are acoustically optimized to complement the natural response of the AV subwoofers, enhancing punch, clarity and extension.

![](https://static.audison.com/media/2025/09/audison_avII-subwoofers-box-tech2.jpg)

### Robust Construction and Resonance Control

Each enclosure is built from 15 millimetres, 0.59-inch, high density MDF panels, chosen for their damping properties and structural integrity. Internal bracing is strategically placed to reduce panel resonances and vibrations, preserving the precision and tonal definition of the bass output.

This results in a clean, well-controlled response that integrates seamlessly with front stage components, maintaining sonic coherence across the entire system.

![](https://static.audison.com/media/2025/09/audison_avII-subwoofers-box-tech3.jpg)

### Innovative Connectivity and Installation Versatility

A standout feature of the VOCE II boxes is the Sub Smart Plug, SSP, terminal, which combines Plug and Play quick release functionality with traditional push terminals. This dual mode connection system streamlines installation and simplifies serviceability while supporting high current applications.

To further enhance adaptability, the enclosures adopt the 2S² dual orientation design, allowing for Down Firing installation, ideal for deeper bass extension and concealment, or Up Firing positioning to maximize punch and impact.

The result is a fully integrated subwoofer solution that offers VOCE II level sonic performance, installation flexibility and a refined aesthetic, elevating the low frequency foundation of any high-end car audio system.
